Kenya 7-day weather forecast

Prefer the version without animation? Open the standard dashboard.

Each ward’s value is the forecast at its centre. A county’s value is the average of its wards. Each forecast day runs from 09:00 EAT to 09:00 EAT the next day.

The alert counts use 10 mm or more of rain, a maximum of 33 °C or above, and a minimum of 10 °C or below, based on each area’s average.
